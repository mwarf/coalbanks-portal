## What Happened

The Essex Charity Golf Classic on September 9 closed out capture for the 40th Anniversary project. Everything the film still needed came home from that day, and more than we had planned for.

All of the September material is now ingested, organized and transcribed. That means the edit starts from selects rather than from a logging pass, which is the part that usually eats the time later.

### What We Came Home With

**Employee interviews** came together. Pam Anderson, Shelagh Benning, and Jeff from the ATB side all sat down, which fills the biggest gap the film had.

**The founder told his own story.** Russ sat for a full interview. His account of leaving the bank in 1986, the first deal, and the month the company made $500 is now on camera in his own words. We also captured Russ and Ross together for the baton-pass moment.

**The evening program** is covered end to end, including Kids Cancer Care and the spokeskid speech.

**The tournament itself** is fully covered: 216 golfers, 27 holes at Heritage Pointe, drone and ground footage, the awards, and the auction.

### What It Changes in the Film

Three things improved because of that day. Russ now opens the film rather than being described by someone else. The community act has an ending it did not have before, thanks to the spokeskid speech at the dinner. And the people section is filled with real employee voices, from long-tenure staff to a recent hire from the ATB acquisition.
