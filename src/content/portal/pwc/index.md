## Project Scope

**One film, 45 to 90 seconds, 16:9, for the Town of Taber and the MD of Taber.**

Progressive West Consulting awarded Coalbanks this project on November 16, 2026. The film presents Taber Region as a place to build and grow an agricultural business, supporting the economic development work led in partnership by the Town of Taber and the MD of Taber.

The five value propositions the film carries:

- Irrigation infrastructure, and the diversity and number of crops grown in the region
- Availability and price of land, distinguishing farm, industrial and light commercial opportunities
- Decreased red tape, framed as coordinated access to the right people early
- The partnership between the Town of Taber and the MD of Taber
- Regional accessibility, including Highway 3, Highway 36, rail and the border crossing

### Style and Use

Polished corporate register, built for investor presentations, boardroom use, digital advertising and general promotional material, including Meta and LinkedIn. General enough to speak to investment attraction and to workforce recruitment at the same time. Confident narration, clear factual graphics, energetic music, and strong regional and aerial imagery.

### How We Handle the Season

Winter limits what can be filmed authentically in the fields, so the agriculture story is carried by cleared archive and partner footage. The business, processing, transport and municipal subjects are filmed on the day. Any AI-assisted treatment comes to you for approval first, and would never present invented infrastructure as documentary footage.
