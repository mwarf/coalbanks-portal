## What You're Watching

This is the first cut of the 90 second brand anthem, built on the edit plan you and Dell approved on August 21. It is a people-led film: your clients and team carry the story in their own words, rather than a voiceover reading a script over top.

The edit moves from everyday life at home, through the people and care behind the build, and out into the community. Brent's "5,000 hands" line is the anchor. The coffee transition from the edit plan is in as well, cutting between cup details in two different homes.

The film closes on **"You deserve better. Everywhere."**

## Before You Watch

Two things are still in progress, so you can skip past them:

- **Music and sound design** are still rough and will be refined for the next pass.
- **On-screen graphics** are placeholder type and colour. Once we have your logo files, brand typeface, and colour values, we can lock the graphics to your brand.

## What to Look For

- Does the overall story hold together across the 90 seconds?
- Are these the right interview selections and the right moments from each person?
- Does the film sit right between home, craftsmanship, and community, or does one of the three need more room?
